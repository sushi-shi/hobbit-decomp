# Source and license scope

The CC0 1.0 declaration in `LICENSE` applies to project-authored tooling and
documentation and to donor material already released under CC0. It does not
apply to imported game/engine source, third-party libraries, SDK headers,
compiler headers, original binaries or media.

The tooling derives from local Gruntz and HoMM1. Donor revisions are recorded
in `config/tooling-donor.json` and tool module comments; the retained donor
license is `scripts/DONOR-LICENSE`.

Imported Entropy source retains original copyright notices. The sibling trees
have no established project-wide license; their presence does not assert that
they are CC0. Reconstructed game source is not covered by the tooling license.

Third-party components retain their individual terms. Preserved notices
include `LICENSES/`, inline source/header notices and the
provenance in `vendor/msvc-stl-sp5/README.md`.
